What a certificate tells you

  • Carat weight — the diamond's weight to the hundredth of a carat.
  • Colour grade — assessed against master stones under standardised lighting.
  • Clarity grade — the nature, size and position of inclusions at 10× magnification.
  • Cut grade — how well the diamond interacts with light (round brilliant only).
  • Measurements, polish, symmetry, fluorescence — additional quality indicators.
